## Placeholders

`placeholder_*.png` are generated by `scripts/figures/portrait_placeholders.py`
for people who have no freely licensed photograph. Only Alonzo Church's
is in use; the Gonthier and Manners placeholders were dropped on
2026-10-16.

They are silhouettes with initials, not invented likenesses. To replace one,
drop the real photograph in as `<surname>.jpg`, point the chapter at it and
add the credit line above; nothing else changes.

## Permissions

Photographs of Commelin and Nash exist on the Mathlib Initiative team page
and of Massot on his own homepage, but all are under default copyright.
Permission to use one in this course was requested from all three by e-mail
on 2026-10-15.

- Johan Commelin -- **granted**, 2026-10-15. In use as `commelin.jpg`.
- Patrick Massot -- **granted**, 2026-10-15. In use as `massot.jpg`.
- Oliver Nash -- **granted**, 2026-10-15. In use as `nash.jpg`.
- Floris van Doorn -- **granted**, 2026-10-15. In use as `vandoorn.jpg`.

All four who were asked said yes.

The Buzzard photograph replaced the 1996 Wikimedia headshot by George
Bergman (CC BY-SA 4.0,
<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kevin_Buzzard_1996_(headshot).jpg>),
which was free but thirty years old.

Asking was necessary even though the Lean community website is MIT licensed.
A repository licence can carry the photographer's copyright at most; the
depicted person's own right in their likeness is a separate right that the
photographer cannot license away, and consent to appear in a maintainer list
is not consent to appear in teaching material. The portraits on that site are
also only 100x100 pixels, below every photograph used here.
